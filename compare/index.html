📲 Use CartCompare like an app: open your browser's share menu and tap Add to Home Screen.

Your grocery list

One item per line. Quantities work too — try “2x chicken breast”, “bananas x3”, or “2 milk”.

Import a live cart

Already have a cart going at Whole Foods, Target, or Instacart? Copy your cart and paste it below — we'll pull out the items, quantities, and the exact prices you're being charged, so that store's column shows your real prices, not estimates.

On your phone: open the cart in your browser (not the store's app), press and hold to start selecting, drag the handles across your items, copy, and paste here. It doesn't need to be tidy — a rough selection with extra page junk is fine, we filter it out.
On a computer: open the cart page, select everything (Ctrl/Cmd‑A), copy, and paste here.

Cart totals by store

Ranked cheapest first. A store must carry every matched item to win “Cheapest full cart”.

📍 Comparing all six chains — including ones that may not be near you. Enter your ZIP to compare only the stores in your area.

Line-by-line comparison

The cheapest store for each item is highlighted. Tap any price to adjust it — you'll also get a “check ↗” button that opens that store's own search for the item (on your phone it opens their app or site, showing your local store's real price). Type in what you see and totals update instantly. Prices marked ● are yours.

Prices are representative estimates for a typical U.S. metro area (updated August 2026), not live store data. Actual prices vary by location, brand, and sales — tap any price to correct it for your area.

🔒 Your list never leaves your phone. Everything — your list, cart imports, and edited prices — is stored only on your device. The one exception: your ZIP code is sent to OpenStreetMap (a non-profit map service) when you tap “Find my stores.”
